## 洛伦兹变换 $S\to S^\prime$

正变换:

$$
\begin{aligned}
x^\prime &= \dfrac{x-vt}{\sqrt{1-\left(\dfrac{v}{c}\right)^2}} \\
y^\prime &= y \\
z^\prime &= z \\
t^\prime &= \dfrac{t-\dfrac{vx}{c^2}}{\sqrt{1-\left(\dfrac{v}{c}\right)^2}}
\end{aligned}
$$

逆变换:

$$
\begin{aligned}
x &= \dfrac{x^\prime + vt^\prime}{\sqrt{1-\left(\dfrac{v}{c}\right)^2}} \\
y &= y^\prime \\
z &= z^\prime \\
t &= \dfrac{t^\prime + \dfrac{vx^\prime}{c^2}}{\sqrt{1-\left(\dfrac{v}{c}\right)^2}}
\end{aligned}
$$
